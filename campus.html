    Campus crime reports

    Colleges and universities that take federal student aid must report each year how many crimes were reported to campus officials and police, by kind of crime and by place (on campus, residence halls, noncampus buildings, public property). The U.S. Department of Education publishes the counts. Pick a school to see its counts for a year, how they changed from its earliest to its latest report, and the trend.

    These are counts of crimes reported, not all crime, and schools are not comparable. Read the cautions before you use a number; they are repeated beside every table.
