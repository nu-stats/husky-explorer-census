Agency report card

Pick a police department to see what it sent the FBI’s incident-based system (NIBRS) each year, how that compares with the other departments in its state, what the FBI published for it, and what is worth asking about.

“All agencies” lists every agency in the FBI’s files, including metropolitan departments such as Las Vegas Metropolitan Police, county departments such as Miami-Dade, sheriffs and campuses; each is compared with agencies of the same type in its state. The other two choices list only city and town departments matched to a census place by name.

Choose a state, then a department.
