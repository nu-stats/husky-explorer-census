Comparison

This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau.

Health measures: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, PLACES 2025 release (public domain). Modeled small-area estimates of the percent of adults, not counts; no margin of error is given, so no “differs” test is run on them.

Environment: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Environmental Public Health Tracking Network (www.cdc.gov/ephtracking). Air quality is the EPA Downscaler model, a 2022 average; park access is 2020. Interpretations here are not CDC’s.

Change since 2010: U.S. Census Bureau, 2010 and 2020 Censuses. County values compare the two counts directly; tract values are estimates made by carrying 2010 counts onto 2020 tracts through the Census Bureau’s block relationship files (method after Logan, Xu and Stults 2014); no margin of error is given.

Life expectancy: National Center for Health Statistics, U.S. Small-area Life Expectancy Estimates Project (USALEEP), 2010–2015, on 2010 tract boundaries; tracts redrawn since then have no value.

Margins of error are at the 90 percent confidence level. “Differs” means the two estimates differ by more than their margins of error allow, by the Census Bureau’s test; it is not shown when a margin is not published (a 2020 Census count is exact, and ACS population totals are controlled to official estimates).
