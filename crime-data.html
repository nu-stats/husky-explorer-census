    Using crime data

    Husky Explorer shows crime numbers from the FBI’s Uniform Crime Reporting program because they are free, national and go down to the police department. They are also easy to misuse. This page says what they are, then points to the research that explains their limits and to work that has used them, from people other than the FBI.
