    Data sources and credits
    
        Health measures: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, PLACES 2025 release (public domain). These are modeled small-area estimates of the percent of adults, not counts.
        
    
        Change since 2010: U.S. Census Bureau, 2010 and 2020 Censuses. County values compare the two counts directly; tract values are estimates made by carrying 2010 counts onto 2020 tracts through the Census Bureau’s block relationship files (method after Logan, Xu and Stults 2014).
        
    
        Life expectancy: National Center for Health Statistics, U.S. Small-area Life Expectancy Estimates Project (USALEEP), 2010–2015, on 2010 tract boundaries; tracts redrawn since then have no value.
        
    
        Environment: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Environmental Public Health Tracking Network (www.cdc.gov/ephtracking). Air quality is the EPA Downscaler model, a 2022 average; park access is 2020. Interpretations here are not CDC’s.
        
    
        Street map: © OpenStreetMap contributors (openstreetmap.org/copyright), shown faded under the data and optional; turning it off stops all requests to OpenStreetMap.
        
    
        Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau, 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000), and 2010 cartographic boundary files for the 2010 census; water-only tracts are not drawn. Colors from ColorBrewer (Brewer, Harrower and Penn State). Margins of error are at the 90 percent confidence level.
