Find a place

New England towns and the townships of states such as New York, Pennsylvania and Michigan are “towns and townships”; incorporated cities and census-designated places are “cities and places”. A city can be in both lists.

Place profile

Choose a state and a kind of area, then type a name. You get the place at a glance and every table the Census Bureau publishes for it that has been fetched here, with margins of error.

Tables

Pick one or many tables. Click picks one; ctrl-click (Cmd-click on a Mac) adds or removes one; shift-click takes everything from the last click. Arrow keys, Space and Ctrl+A work too.
