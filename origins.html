This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau.

Countries of birth: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 5-year 2020–2024, table B05006. Margins of error are at the 90 percent confidence level; small groups have wide margins, and a whole state adds its counties’ margins by the Bureau’s rule for sums.

Refugees and asylum-seekers: UNHCR Refugee Population Statistics Database (api.unhcr.org/population/v1), CC BY 4.0. These are people under UNHCR’s mandate counted by country of origin, not all immigrants, and the national figures are not broken down by place within the United States.
